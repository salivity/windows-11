Enable or Disable Windows 11 Microsoft Store Updates

By default, Windows 11 automatically downloads and installs updates for all apps acquired through the Microsoft Store. While this ensures you always have the latest features and security patches, you may want to turn this feature off to conserve bandwidth, prevent unexpected app interface changes, or manage disk space manually. This guide provides a straightforward walkthrough on how to toggle automatic app updates on or off in Windows 11, along with instructions for manually updating your apps when needed.

How to Toggle Automatic App Updates

Follow these steps to turn automatic updates on or off:

  1. Open the Microsoft Store: Click the Start button, type Microsoft Store, and press Enter (or select its icon from your taskbar).
  2. Access Settings: Click on your Profile icon located in the top-right corner of the window, then select Settings from the drop-down menu.
  3. Toggle App Updates: At the top of the Settings page, locate the App updates option.
    • Switch the toggle to Off to disable automatic app updates.
    • Switch the toggle to On to enable automatic app updates.

The changes take effect immediately without requiring a system restart.

How to Manually Update Apps When Auto-Update Is Disabled

If you turn off automatic updates, you will need to check for and install updates manually to keep your apps secure and functional:

  1. Open the Microsoft Store.
  2. Click on the Library icon located in the bottom-left corner of the window.
  3. Click the Get updates button at the top right.
  4. The Store will scan for available updates. You can then click Update all to install every available update, or click the individual download icon next to a specific app.
