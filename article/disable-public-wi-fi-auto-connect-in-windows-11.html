Disable Public Wi-Fi Auto-Connect in Windows 11

Connecting automatically to open public Wi-Fi hotspots exposes your device to serious security and privacy risks, including data interception and malware. This guide provides clear, step-by-step methods to disable automatic open hotspot connections and prevent Windows 11 from joining unsecured networks without your explicit permission.

Method 1: Disable Auto-Connect for Known Public Networks

If your computer has previously connected to an open network, Windows 11 will attempt to reconnect whenever that network is within range. You can disable this behavior directly in Settings.

  1. Open the Settings app by pressing Windows Key + I.
  2. Select Network & internet from the left sidebar.
  3. Click on Wi-Fi on the right pane.
  4. Click on Manage known networks.
  5. Locate the open or public network from the list and click on it.
  6. Uncheck the box labeled Connect automatically when in range, or click Forget to remove the network entirely.

Method 2: Turn Off Hotspot 2.0 Networks

Windows 11 includes Hotspot 2.0 features that automatically connect your device to participating public Wi-Fi providers. Disabling this prevents your system from associating with these networks automatically.

  1. Open Settings (Windows Key + I).
  2. Navigate to Network & internet > Wi-Fi.
  3. Scroll down and click on Manage known networks or open the Wi-Fi preferences section.
  4. Locate the Hotspot 2.0 networks option (sometimes listed as Let me use Hotspot 2.0 networks to connect).
  5. Toggle the switch to Off.

Method 3: Disable Auto-Connect When Connecting to New Networks

To prevent future public networks from connecting automatically:

  1. Click the Network icon on your taskbar (near the clock) to open Quick Settings.
  2. Click the arrow next to the Wi-Fi icon to view available networks.
  3. Select an open network you need to use temporarily.
  4. Uncheck the box next to Connect automatically before clicking Connect.
