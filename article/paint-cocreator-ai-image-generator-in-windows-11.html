Paint Cocreator: AI Image Generator in Windows 11

Paint Cocreator is an AI-powered image generation feature integrated directly into the classic Microsoft Paint application for Windows 11. Powered by OpenAI's DALL-E model, the tool enables users to create original artwork, illustrations, and photorealistic designs from simple text descriptions. This article covers what Paint Cocreator is, how its underlying technology functions, and the exact steps required to generate AI artwork on your PC.

What is Paint Cocreator?

Paint Cocreator is a native Windows 11 feature that brings generative artificial intelligence to the Paint app. Instead of drawing from scratch, users can type a natural language prompt, choose an artistic style, and receive AI-generated images within seconds.

The feature relies on a credit-based system. Users receive an initial balance of 50 credits to generate images upon signing in with a Microsoft account. Each generation request consumes one credit.

How Paint Cocreator Generates Images

Paint Cocreator operates using a cloud-based text-to-image neural network:

  1. Prompt Processing: When you enter a text description, Paint sends the text along with your chosen style to Microsoft's cloud servers.
  2. AI Diffusion Model: The DALL-E model interprets the semantic meaning of your words, identifying subjects, colors, lighting, composition, and mood.
  3. Image Synthesis: The model generates three unique image variations based on the prompt.
  4. Canvas Integration: Selecting any generated variation immediately inserts the image onto your Paint canvas, where it can be modified with standard Paint tools, layers, and background removal features.

Requirements to Use Paint Cocreator

  • Operating System: Windows 11 (latest build recommended).
  • Application: Updated version of Microsoft Paint (via the Microsoft Store).
  • Account: A signed-in Microsoft account.
  • Internet Connection: Active connection required for cloud-based AI processing.

How to Generate AI Images with Paint Cocreator

  1. Open Paint: Launch Microsoft Paint from the Start menu or Windows search bar.
  2. Access Cocreator: Click the Cocreator icon located on the right side of the top toolbar. A side panel will open.
  3. Enter a Prompt: In the text box, describe what you want to create with as much detail as possible (e.g., "a futuristic city at sunset with flying cars").
  4. Choose a Style: Click the style dropdown menu to select a visual preset, such as Digital Art, Oil Painting, Watercolor, Pixel Art, or Anime. You can also choose None to let the prompt dictate the style.
  5. Click Create: Select the Create button. Paint Cocreator will process your request and display three generated variations in the side panel.
  6. Apply to Canvas: Click on your preferred image to add it directly to your drawing canvas for further editing, resizing, or saving.
