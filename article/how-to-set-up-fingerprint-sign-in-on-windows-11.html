How to Set Up Fingerprint Sign-In on Windows 11

Setting up fingerprint sign-in on Windows 11 allows you to unlock your PC quickly and securely using Windows Hello biometrics. This guide explains how to enable and configure a built-in or external biometric fingerprint scanner, register your fingerprints, and manage your sign-in preferences directly through Windows Settings.

Prerequisites

Before configuring fingerprint authentication, ensure you have:

  • A Windows 11-compatible fingerprint reader (built-in or USB).
  • The latest biometric drivers installed via Windows Update or your device manufacturer.
  • A Windows Hello PIN already set up on your account.

Step-by-Step Configuration

Step 1: Open Sign-in Options

  1. Press Windows Key + I to open the Settings app.
  2. In the left navigation pane, select Accounts.
  3. In the right pane, click on Sign-in options.

Step 2: Access Fingerprint Recognition

  1. Under the Ways to sign in section, locate and click on Fingerprint recognition (Windows Hello) to expand the menu.
  2. Click the Set up button. If the button is not visible or grayed out, ensure your scanner is connected and that you have configured a Windows account PIN.

Step 3: Complete the Windows Hello Setup

  1. A setup window will appear. Click Get started.
  2. Enter your current account PIN when prompted to verify your identity.
  3. Place your finger on the fingerprint sensor, lift it, and touch it repeatedly at slightly different angles as prompted on the screen until the progress bar completes.
  4. Click Close once the scan is complete.

Adding Additional Fingerprints

To ensure you can unlock your device using either hand or multiple fingers:

  1. Return to Settings > Accounts > Sign-in options.
  2. Click Fingerprint recognition (Windows Hello).
  3. Click Add another finger and repeat the scanning process with a different finger.

Removing Fingerprint Sign-In

If you no longer wish to use biometric sign-in:

  1. Go to Settings > Accounts > Sign-in options.
  2. Expand Fingerprint recognition (Windows Hello).
  3. Click Remove. Your fingerprint data will be deleted immediately, and your account will revert to PIN or password authentication.
