How to Calibrate ClearType in Windows 11

ClearType is a built-in Windows utility designed to make on-screen text appear sharper, smoother, and easier to read on flat-panel monitors. This guide provides a straightforward, step-by-step walkthrough on how to open the ClearType Text Tuner in Windows 11 and calibrate your display settings to eliminate blurry fonts and reduce eye strain.

Step 1: Open the ClearType Text Tuner

  1. Click the Start button or press the Windows Key.
  2. Type ClearType or Adjust ClearType text into the search bar.
  3. Select Adjust ClearType text from the search results to launch the wizard.

Alternatively, you can open it via the Run dialog:

  1. Press Windows Key + R.
  2. Type cttune and press Enter.

Step 2: Enable ClearType

  1. In the ClearType Text Tuner window, ensure the box next to Turn on ClearType is checked.
  2. Click Next to proceed.

Step 3: Select Your Monitor

  1. If you use multiple monitors, the wizard will ask if you want to tune all monitors or only a specific one. Choose your preferred option and click Next.
  2. Windows will verify that your display is set to its native resolution. If the resolution is correct, click Next.

Step 4: Choose the Best-Looking Text Samples

The tuner will present a series of five screens, each displaying several blocks of sample text:

  1. Look closely at the text blocks presented on the screen.
  2. Click on the text sample that appears cleanest, sharpest, and easiest to read for your eyes.
  3. Click Next after making each selection.
  4. Repeat this process for all five rounds.

If you have multiple displays, you will repeat these steps for each connected screen.

Step 5: Finish and Apply Settings

  1. Once you complete the selections for all monitors, click Finish.
  2. Your new font-rendering settings are applied immediately across the operating system without requiring a restart.
