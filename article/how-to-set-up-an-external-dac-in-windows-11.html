How to Set Up an External DAC in Windows 11

Connecting an external Digital-to-Analog Converter (DAC) or soundcard to a Windows 11 PC significantly improves audio quality for headphones and speakers. This guide walks you through the entire process, from making the physical connection and installing drivers to configuring audio sample rates and enabling exclusive mode in Windows 11 settings.

1. Connect the DAC or Soundcard

  1. Plug the DAC or external soundcard into an available USB port on your PC using the provided cable (preferably directly into the motherboard or PC chassis rather than an unpowered USB hub).
  2. If your device requires external power, connect its power adapter and turn the device on.
  3. Connect your headphones, powered speakers, or amplifier to the output jacks on the DAC.

2. Install Drivers (If Necessary)

Most modern USB DACs are USB Audio Class 2 (UAC2) compliant and work automatically using standard Windows 11 plug-and-play drivers.

If your DAC manufacturer provides proprietary drivers (such as ASIO drivers for low latency and bit-perfect playback):

  1. Visit the manufacturer’s official support or downloads page.
  2. Download and install the latest Windows 11 driver package.
  3. Restart your computer if prompted.

3. Set the DAC as the Default Output Device

  1. Open Settings by pressing Win + I.
  2. Navigate to System > Sound.
  3. Under the Output section, locate the Choose where to play sound list.
  4. Select your external DAC or soundcard to set it as the active audio output.

4. Configure Sample Rate and Bit Depth

To get the highest audio resolution from your DAC:

  1. In Settings > System > Sound, click the arrow (>) next to your DAC under the Output section to open its Properties.
  2. Locate the Output settings section.
  3. Click the dropdown menu next to Format.
  4. Select the highest bit depth and sample rate supported by your DAC and media files (e.g., 24 bit, 96000 Hz (Studio Quality) or 32 bit, 192000 Hz).
  5. Click Test to ensure audio plays correctly through the device.

5. Configure Advanced Properties and Exclusive Mode

For optimal sound reproduction and bit-perfect streaming from apps like Tidal, Qobuz, or Foobar2000:

  1. Scroll down to the bottom of the DAC's properties page and click More sound settings.
  2. In the classic Sound control panel, double-click your external DAC.
  3. Go to the Enhancements tab (if available) and check Disable all enhancements to prevent Windows from altering the sound signature.
  4. Go to the Advanced tab.
  5. Under Exclusive Mode, ensure both of the following options are checked:
    • Allow applications to take exclusive control of this device
    • Give exclusive mode applications priority
  6. Click Apply, then click OK.
