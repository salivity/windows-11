How to Clear Location History on Windows 11

Windows 11 tracks and stores your physical location to provide relevant features for apps like Maps, Weather, and web browsers. This guide explains how to quickly clear your stored location history directly from your Windows 11 Settings, helping you maintain your digital privacy and remove past location data saved on your device.

Steps to Clear Location History

  1. Open Settings: Press the Windows Key + I shortcut on your keyboard, or click the Start button and select the Settings gear icon.
  2. Go to Privacy & Security: In the left sidebar of the Settings window, click on Privacy & security.
  3. Select Location: Scroll down to the App permissions section and click on Location.
  4. Locate History Settings: Scroll down the Location page until you find the Location history section.
  5. Clear the Data: Click the Clear button next to Clear history on this device.

Once clicked, a checkmark will appear next to the button to confirm that your local location history has been deleted.

How to Prevent Windows 11 from Storing Location History

If you want to stop Windows 11 from saving your location in the future, you can manage your location permissions in the same menu:

  • Disable Location Services Completely: Toggle the Location services switch to Off at the top of the Location settings page. This prevents Windows and all apps from accessing your physical location.
  • Manage Individual App Permissions: If you want to keep location services active for specific apps (like Maps) but block others, leave the main switch On and toggle off access for individual applications listed under Let apps access your location.
