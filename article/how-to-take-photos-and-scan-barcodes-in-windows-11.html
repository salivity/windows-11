How to Take Photos and Scan Barcodes in Windows 11

The built-in Camera app in Windows 11 is a versatile tool that allows users to quickly capture photos, record videos, and scan barcodes or QR codes without needing third-party software. This guide covers how to set up your camera permissions, take photos using standard and advanced settings, and use the integrated scanning features to read codes instantly using your device's webcam.

Opening the Camera App

  1. Press the Windows Key on your keyboard or click the Start button.
  2. Type Camera into the search bar and select the Camera application from the results.
  3. If prompted, grant the app permission to access your camera and microphone by navigating to Settings > Privacy & security > Camera and toggling the permissions to On.

How to Capture Photos

  1. Select Photo Mode: Once the Camera app opens, locate the mode selector on the right sidebar and click the Photo icon (represented by a still camera).
  2. Adjust Photo Settings (Optional):
    • Timer: Click the stopwatch icon to set a delay (2, 5, or 10 seconds).
    • HDR and Quality: Click the Settings (gear) icon in the top-left corner to adjust photo quality, aspect ratio, and framing grids.
    • Brightness/Exposure: Use the slider on the left to manually adjust exposure if the lighting is too dark or bright.
  3. Take the Picture: Click the large white Camera button on the right side of the window, or press the Spacebar on your keyboard.
  4. View Your Photos: The captured photo will appear as a thumbnail in the bottom-right corner. All images are automatically saved to your computer in the Pictures > Camera Roll folder.

How to Scan Barcodes and QR Codes

  1. Switch to Barcode Mode: In the Camera app, look at the right-hand panel and click the Barcode icon (resembling a square QR code/barcode).
    • Note: If the barcode icon is missing, click the Settings (gear) icon, expand Related settings, and toggle on Try out experimental features or ensure the latest Windows updates are installed.
  2. Position the Code: Hold the QR code or barcode steadily in front of your camera, centering it within the on-screen framing guides.
  3. Read the Result: Once the camera recognizes the pattern, it will automatically detect the data and display a clickable link or text snippet on the screen.
  4. Access the Content: Click the link directly to open it in your default web browser, or copy the decoded text to your clipboard.
