Back Up BitLocker Key to Microsoft Account in Windows 11

This guide explains how to back up your BitLocker recovery key directly to your Microsoft cloud account in Windows 11. Storing this 48-digit key in the cloud ensures you maintain access to your encrypted drive if your device encounters hardware changes, firmware updates, or startup errors.

Prerequisites

  • A Windows 11 edition that supports BitLocker (Pro, Enterprise, or Education).
  • An active Microsoft account signed in to the device with administrative privileges.
  • BitLocker encryption already enabled on the target drive.

Steps to Back Up the Recovery Key

  1. Open BitLocker Management:

    • Press the Windows Key or click Start.
    • Type Manage BitLocker in the search bar and select the matching Control Panel result.

  2. Access Backup Options:

    • Locate the encrypted drive (usually the Operating System Drive C:).
    • Click on the Back up your recovery key link next to the drive status.

  3. Choose the Microsoft Account Option:

    • In the pop-up window, select Save to your Microsoft account.
    • Wait a few seconds for Windows to upload the key to the cloud.

  4. Confirm Completion:

    • A message will confirm that your recovery key has been saved. Click Finish.

How to Verify Your Saved Key

To confirm that the recovery key was successfully backed up:

  1. Open a web browser and navigate to your Microsoft Account device recovery page (account.microsoft.com/devices/recoverykey).
  2. Sign in with the same Microsoft account used on your Windows 11 PC.
  3. Locate your device name in the list to view the matching Key ID and 48-digit BitLocker Recovery Key.
