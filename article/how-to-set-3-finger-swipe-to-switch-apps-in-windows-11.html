How to Set 3-Finger Swipe to Switch Apps in Windows 11

Customizing touchpad gestures in Windows 11 can significantly improve your multitasking workflow. This guide provides a straightforward walkthrough on how to configure three-finger swipe gestures on a precision touchpad to switch between active tasks, cycle through open windows, and view your running applications.

Requirements

To use multi-finger gestures, your Windows 11 laptop must be equipped with a Precision Touchpad. Most modern Windows laptops include this feature by default.

Step-by-Step Configuration

  1. Open Windows Settings
    Press Win + I on your keyboard or click the Start button and select Settings.

  2. Navigate to Touchpad Settings
    In the left sidebar, click Bluetooth & devices. Then, scroll down on the right pane and select Touchpad.

  3. Locate Three-Finger Gestures
    Scroll down the Touchpad settings page and click on Three-finger gestures to expand the section.

  4. Set Swipes to Switch Apps
    Click the dropdown menu next to Swipes and select Switch apps and show desktop.

How the Default Gestures Work

Once enabled, your three-finger swipes will control your active tasks as follows:

  • Swipe Left / Right: Instantly cycles between your active applications.
  • Swipe Up: Opens Task View, allowing you to see all open windows and virtual desktops.
  • Swipe Down: Minimizes all windows to reveal the desktop (swipe up to restore them).

Customizing Individual Swipe Directions (Advanced)

If you prefer custom actions for individual swipe directions:

  1. On the Touchpad settings page, scroll down and click Advanced gestures.
  2. Under the Three-finger gestures section, manually assign your preferred action to Swipe left and Swipe right by selecting Switch apps or Previous/Next app.
