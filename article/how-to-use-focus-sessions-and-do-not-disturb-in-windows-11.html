How to Use Focus Sessions and Do Not Disturb in Windows 11

Windows 11 includes integrated productivity tools designed to minimize distractions and improve workflow management: Focus sessions and Do Not Disturb. This guide explains how to enable, configure, and customize these features to help you manage your time effectively, silence unwanted alerts, and maintain concentration while working on your PC.

Understanding the Difference

  • Do Not Disturb (DND): Automatically silences incoming notification banners and sends them directly to the Notification Center.
  • Focus Sessions: A dedicated productivity mode that turns on Do Not Disturb, disables taskbar app flashing, and launches a countdown timer with optional integration with Microsoft To Do and Spotify.

How to Use Do Not Disturb

Quick Toggle via the Notification Center

  1. Click the Date and Time area in the bottom-right corner of the taskbar (or press Win + N).
  2. Click the bell icon in the top-right corner of the notification pane to turn Do Not Disturb on or off.

Automatic Scheduling via Settings

  1. Open Settings (Win + I) and navigate to System > Notifications.
  2. Toggle Do not disturb to On manually, or expand the Turn on do not disturb automatically section.
  3. Check the conditions you prefer, such as:
    • During specific time ranges (e.g., during work hours).
    • When duplicating your display for presentations.
    • When playing a full-screen game.
    • When using an app in full-screen mode.

Setting Priority Notifications

To ensure critical alerts still come through while Do Not Disturb is active:

  1. Go to Settings > System > Notifications.
  2. Click Set priority notifications.
  3. Customize which calls, reminders, and specific apps are allowed to bypass the silence filter.

How to Start and Customize Focus Sessions

Quick Start from the Taskbar

  1. Click the Date and Time on the taskbar (Win + N).
  2. Look below the calendar to find the Focus widget.
  3. Use the + or – buttons to choose your session duration (e.g., 30 minutes).
  4. Click Focus to begin.

Using the Clock App for Advanced Features

For deeper customization and task tracking, use the built-in Windows Clock app:

  1. Open the Start menu, search for Clock, and launch the app.
  2. Select Focus sessions from the left navigation bar.
  3. Set your target duration. If you choose a session longer than 30 minutes, the app automatically schedules rest breaks.
  4. Optional: Connect Microsoft To Do to select specific tasks to complete during the session.
  5. Optional: Connect your Spotify account to play focus-oriented music playlists directly within the Clock app.
  6. Click Start focus session.

Customizing Focus Behavior

To adjust how Windows behaves when a Focus session is active:

  1. Open Settings (Win + I) > System > Focus.
  2. Select or deselect your preferred options:
    • Show the timer in the Clock app: Keeps a compact timer widget on your screen.
    • Hide badges on taskbar apps: Removes unread message counters on app icons.
    • Stop flashing on taskbar apps: Prevents background apps from pulsing to get attention.
    • Turn on do not disturb: Automatically silences standard alerts for the duration of the session.
  3. When finished, click Start focus session or simply close the Settings window; your preferences will apply to all future sessions.
