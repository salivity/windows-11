Configure Palm Rejection on Windows 11 Tablets

Palm rejection on Windows 11 tablets prevents the touchscreen from registering accidental inputs from your hand while writing or drawing with an active stylus. While Windows 11 does not feature a universal "sensitivity slider" for palm detection, you can effectively configure and optimize palm rejection using native Windows Ink settings, handedness orientation, and device-specific driver utilities.

Enable Native Pen Palm Rejection in Settings

The most effective way to eliminate accidental touches is to tell Windows to ignore all finger and palm touches whenever your stylus is near the screen.

  1. Open Settings by pressing Windows + I.
  2. Select Bluetooth & devices from the left sidebar.
  3. Click on Pen & Windows Ink.
  4. Click on Additional pen settings to expand the menu.
  5. Check the box next to Ignore touch input when I'm using my pen.

This activates active digitizer palm rejection, completely disabling capacitive touch input whenever the tablet detects the pen's proximity.

Set Dominant Hand Orientation

Windows uses your hand preference to predict the angle and area where your palm will rest on the display.

  1. Navigate to Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Pen & Windows Ink.
  2. Locate the Choose which hand you write with dropdown menu.
  3. Select either Right hand or Left hand.

Setting this correctly ensures the operating system ignores touch data in the specific quadrant where your palm naturally rests.

Adjust Touch Sensitivity and Multi-Finger Gestures

If stray touches continue to register, disabling three- and four-finger gestures can reduce overall touch responsiveness to accidental broad inputs.

  1. Go to Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Touch.
  2. Click on Three- and four-finger touch gestures.
  3. Toggle the setting to Off or customize them to prevent accidental multi-touch triggers from the edge of your palm.

Configure Device-Specific Digitizer Apps

Many Windows 11 tablets use proprietary digitizers from manufacturers like Microsoft, Wacom, or ELAN, which provide dedicated software for fine-tuning touch thresholds.

  • Microsoft Surface Devices: Open the pre-installed Surface app, navigate to Pen, and adjust the pen pressure sensitivity curve. Higher sensitivity requires less physical force, reducing the need to rest your hand heavily on the screen.
  • Wacom/Feel Drivers: Open the Wacom Tablet Properties or Wacom Pen app from the Start menu. Navigate to the Touch Options or Pen tab to adjust palm tracking area sizes and touch response delay.
  • Lenovo/Dell/HP Utility Apps: Check apps like Lenovo Vantage or HP Pen Control for active stylus proximity detection settings.

Recalibrate Screen for Pen and Touch Input

If palm rejection coordinates are misaligned, recalibrating the screen resets the touch grid.

  1. Press Windows + S and type Control Panel.
  2. Set the view to Large icons and select Tablet PC Settings.
  3. Under the Display tab, click Calibrate.
  4. Select Pen input to map the digitizer coordinates precisely to your stylus tip.
