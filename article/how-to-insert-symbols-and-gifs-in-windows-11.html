How to Insert Symbols and GIFs in Windows 11

The Windows 11 Emoji Panel is a built-in tool that allows you to quickly insert standard emojis, animated GIFs, kaomoji, and special characters into any text field. By using a simple keyboard shortcut, you can access an extensive library of symbols and media without needing to copy and paste them from external websites or memorize complex Alt codes.

How to Open the Emoji Panel

  1. Click on any text field, document, or app where you want to insert a character (e.g., Word, browser search bar, Notepad, or a chat app).
  2. Press Windows Key + . (period) or Windows Key + ; (semicolon) on your keyboard.
  3. The Emoji Panel pop-up will appear directly above or near your cursor.

How to Insert Special Characters and Symbols

The symbol section includes punctuation marks, currency symbols, mathematical operators, accented letters, and geometric shapes.

  1. Open the Emoji Panel (Windows Key + .).
  2. Click the Symbols icon (represented by the Greek letter Omega Ω) located in the top navigation bar.
  3. Select a category from the bottom navigation strip, such as:
    • General Punctuation (e.g., em dash —, en dash –, quotation marks)
    • Currency Symbols (e.g., €, £, ¥, ₹)
    • Latin Symbols (e.g., accented characters like é, ñ, ü)
    • Geometric Symbols (e.g., arrows, checkmarks, circles)
    • Math Symbols (e.g., ±, ÷, ≠, ∞)
    • Supplemental Symbols (e.g., copyright ©, registered ®, trademark ™)
  4. Click on the desired symbol to insert it into your active text field. The panel will remember your frequently used symbols in the Most recently used section at the top.

How to Insert Animated GIFs

Windows 11 integrates the Tenor GIF library directly into the panel.

  1. Open the Emoji Panel (Windows Key + .).
  2. Click the GIF icon (the second icon from the left in the top navigation bar).
  3. Type a keyword or emotion into the search bar at the top (e.g., "applause", "excited", "cat") to find a specific animation, or browse trending categories.
  4. Click the GIF you want to use. It will be copied to your clipboard or pasted directly into supported applications (such as Microsoft Teams, Discord, or web forms).

How to Insert Emojis and Kaomoji

  1. Open the Emoji Panel (Windows Key + .).
  2. For standard emojis: Click the Smiley Face icon. You can browse by category (Smileys, People, Animals, Food, etc.) or simply begin typing a word while the panel is open to automatically search for matching emojis.
  3. For Japanese Kaomoji: Click the Kaomoji icon (represented by ;-) ) to choose classic text-based facial expressions categorized by emotions like happy, sad, or surprised.
  4. Click any item to immediately place it into your text.
