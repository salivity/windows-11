Switch Microsoft Account to Local Account Windows 11

Switching from a Microsoft account to a local user account in Windows 11 allows you to log in offline, avoid cloud synchronization, and enhance your privacy. This guide outlines the exact steps needed to convert your existing profile into an offline local account without losing your installed applications, personal files, or settings.

Steps to Switch to a Local Account

  1. Open Windows Settings Press Windows Key + I on your keyboard, or click the Start button and select the Settings gear icon.

  2. Navigate to Account Settings In the left sidebar, click on Accounts, then select the Your info tab on the right pane.

  3. Choose the Local Account Option Scroll down to the Account settings section. Next to the option labeled Microsoft account, click the link that says Sign in with a local account instead.

  4. Confirm Your Identity A prompt will appear asking if you are sure you want to switch. Click Next. You will then be prompted to enter your current Microsoft account password or your Windows Hello PIN to verify your identity.

  5. Set Up Local Account Credentials Enter your desired local credentials:

    • User name: Choose a name for your local profile.
    • New password: Enter a local password (or leave it blank if you prefer no password).
    • Confirm password: Re-type the password.
    • Password hint: Provide a hint to help you remember the password.

    Click Next once filled out.

  6. Complete the Switch Click Sign out and finish. Windows will log you out of your current session.

Once the screen reloads, log back in using your newly created local username and password. All of your personal files, desktop icons, and configurations will remain intact.

Important Considerations After Switching

  • OneDrive Syncing: Automatic OneDrive backups may pause or require you to sign in manually within the OneDrive app.
  • Microsoft Store & Xbox Apps: Apps requiring a Microsoft identity will ask you to sign in on an app-by-app basis without linking your entire Windows login.
  • BitLocker Recovery: If device encryption is enabled, ensure you have backed up your BitLocker recovery key manually before disconnecting from your Microsoft account.
