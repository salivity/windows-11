How to Restore Windows 11 from a System Image

Restoring Windows 11 from a system image allows you to revert your entire computer—including the operating system, installed programs, drivers, system settings, and personal files—to an exact point in time. This guide outlines the straightforward, step-by-step process to perform a full system recovery using the Windows 11 Advanced Startup environment.

Prerequisites Before You Begin

  • Connect the external hard drive or USB drive containing your system image backup to your PC.
  • If you are using a laptop, ensure it is plugged into a power source to prevent interruption during the restoration process.
  • Back up any new files created since the image backup was made, as the restore process will completely overwrite the target drive.

Step 1: Access Advanced Startup

You can access the recovery environment depending on whether your computer can boot into Windows or not.

Option A: If Your PC Boots into Windows 11

  1. Open Settings by pressing Win + I.
  2. Go to System > Recovery.
  3. Under the Recovery options section, locate Advanced startup and click Restart now.
  4. Confirm by clicking Restart now again.

Option B: If Your PC Cannot Boot into Windows 11

  1. Insert a Windows 11 bootable USB drive or recovery drive and turn on the computer.
  2. Press the appropriate key (usually F12, F11, F8, or Del) to open the boot menu and select the USB drive.
  3. On the Windows Setup screen, select your language preferences and click Next.
  4. Click Repair your computer in the bottom-left corner.

Step 2: Open System Image Recovery

  1. Once your PC reboots into the blue recovery screen, click on Troubleshoot.
  2. Select Advanced options.
  3. Click on See more recovery options (if visible), then select System Image Recovery.
  4. Choose your user account and enter your password if prompted.

Step 3: Select and Apply the System Image

  1. Select Backup: The wizard will automatically detect the most recent system image on your external drive with the option Use the latest available system image (recommended). If you wish to use an older backup, choose Select a system image and manually browse for it.
  2. Click Next.
  3. Configure Options:
    • Check Format and repartition disks if you are restoring to a new hard drive or want to erase everything on the existing partitions.
    • Click Next.
  4. Confirm Restore: Review the details on the final summary screen and click Finish.
  5. When the warning prompt appears asking if you want to continue, click Yes.

The restoration process will begin. This may take anywhere from several minutes to a few hours depending on the size of your backup and drive speed. Once finished, your computer will automatically restart into the restored state of Windows 11.
