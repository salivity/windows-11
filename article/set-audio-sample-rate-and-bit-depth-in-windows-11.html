Set Audio Sample Rate and Bit Depth in Windows 11

Adjusting the default audio sample rate and bit depth in Windows 11 ensures your headphones, speakers, or DAC operate at optimal audio quality. This guide provides a straightforward, step-by-step walkthrough to configure your output format settings using both the modern Windows 11 Settings app and the classic Sound Control Panel.

Method 1: Using Windows 11 Settings

  1. Open Sound Settings: Press Windows Key + I to open the Settings app, navigate to System, and click on Sound.
  2. Select Your Device: Under the Output section, click on the specific playback device (such as your Speakers or Headphones) that you want to configure.
  3. Change the Output Format: Scroll down to the Output settings section and locate the Format drop-down menu.
  4. Choose Sample Rate and Bit Depth: Click the drop-down menu and select your preferred setting (for example, 24 bit, 48000 Hz (Studio Quality) or 24 bit, 96000 Hz).
  5. Test Audio: Click the Test button next to the drop-down menu to ensure audio plays correctly without distortion.

Method 2: Using the Classic Sound Control Panel

  1. Open Run Dialog: Press Windows Key + R to open the Run box.
  2. Launch Sound Panel: Type mmsys.cpl and hit Enter to open the classic Sound Control Panel.
  3. Open Device Properties: In the Playback tab, select your default audio device and click Properties (or double-click the device).
  4. Navigate to Advanced: Click on the Advanced tab at the top of the properties window.
  5. Set Default Format: Under the Default Format section, choose your desired bit depth and sample rate from the drop-down menu.
  6. Apply Changes: Click Test to verify sound output, then click Apply and OK to save the settings.
