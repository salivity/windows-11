Manage Family Safety and Screen Time on Windows 11

Managing screen time and content restrictions on Windows 11 is essential for maintaining healthy digital habits and ensuring online safety for children. Microsoft Family Safety provides a centralized dashboard where parents can connect family accounts, establish daily computer usage schedules, block inappropriate websites and apps, and review weekly activity reports. This guide outlines the exact steps to configure and manage these parental controls on Windows 11 devices.

1. Set Up a Child Account on Windows 11

To apply parental controls, your child must sign in to Windows 11 using their own Microsoft account connected to your family group.

  1. Open Settings on your Windows 11 PC (press Win + I).
  2. Navigate to Accounts > Family.
  3. Click Add someone under the "Your family" section.
  4. Select Create one for a child and follow the on-screen prompts to register or link your child's Microsoft account.
  5. Have your child log into the Windows 11 PC with their credentials to finalize device setup.

2. Configure Screen Time Limits

Once the child account is linked, you can set daily allowances and specific usage windows.

  1. Go to Settings > Accounts > Family and click Manage family settings online, or visit the Microsoft Family Safety website directly.
  2. Select your child’s profile from the dashboard.
  3. In the left navigation menu, click Screen time.
  4. Under the Devices tab, turn on the toggle for Windows 10 and Windows 11.
  5. Select each day of the week to customize:
    • Time limit: The total hours allowed per day (e.g., 2 hours).
    • Schedule: The allowable time window (e.g., between 8:00 AM and 7:00 PM).
  6. Click Done to save the schedule.

3. Set App and Game Limits

You can restrict specific high-use applications, such as games or streaming services, without blocking the entire computer.

  1. Within the child’s profile on the Family Safety dashboard, navigate to Apps and games.
  2. Locate the specific app or game from the usage list.
  3. Click Set app limit next to the application.
  4. Define the maximum allowed daily time for that specific application.

4. Enable Web and Search Filtering

Prevent access to mature websites and enforce safe searching on Microsoft Edge.

  1. In the child's Family Safety dashboard, click Content filters.
  2. Select the Web and search tab.
  3. Turn on Filter inappropriate websites and searches.
  4. (Optional) Under Always allowed or Always blocked, manually enter specific website URLs you want to permit or restrict.

5. Review Activity and Manage Requests

Windows 11 provides tracking features and real-time request handling.

  • Activity Reports: Click Overview in the Family Safety portal to view screen time trends, most-used applications, and visited websites.
  • Extra Time Requests: When your child reaches their time limit, a prompt will appear on their screen allowing them to request more time via email or instant notification to your Family Safety mobile app, which you can approve or decline immediately.
