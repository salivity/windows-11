Windows 11 Win + W Shortcut: Open and Navigate Widgets

The Win + W keyboard shortcut in Windows 11 provides instant access to the Widgets board, a dedicated flyout panel featuring personalized news, weather, calendar events, traffic updates, and system metrics. This guide explains how to trigger the Widgets board using Win + W, navigate its interface using both keyboard and mouse inputs, and manage widget settings efficiently.

How to Open and Close the Widgets Board

Pressing Win + W on your keyboard triggers the following actions:

  • Open: If the Widgets board is closed, pressing Win + W immediately slides the board into view from the left side of the screen (or from the center if taskbar icons are aligned to the left).
  • Toggle/Close: Pressing Win + W a second time while the board is active will dismiss and close it. You can also press the Esc key or click anywhere outside the Widgets board to close it.

Navigating the Widgets Board with the Keyboard

Once opened via Win + W, you can navigate the entire interface without using a mouse:

  • Tab / Shift + Tab: Cycle forward and backward through the interactive elements, including individual widgets, the Add widgets (+) button, user profile settings, and news feed tiles.
  • Arrow Keys: Move focus within multi-element widgets (such as browsing lists or switching between sports scores) or scroll through the news feed.
  • Enter / Spacebar: Activate the selected element, open a widget’s expanded view, or open a linked news article in Microsoft Edge.
  • Menu Key / Shift + F10: Open the context menu (three dots) of a focused widget to access options like resizing, customizing, or unpinning the widget.

Navigating and Interacting with the Mouse

  • Pinning and Unpinning: Click the three-dot menu (...) in the upper-right corner of any widget to resize it (Small, Medium, Large), customize its contents, or unpin it from the board.
  • Rearranging Widgets: Click and drag any widget card by its header to reposition it on your board.
  • Expanding to Full Screen: Click the expand icon (two opposing arrows) in the top-right corner of the Widgets window to view your feed in full-screen mode.

Customizing the Widgets Feed

To modify the content displayed when pressing Win + W:

  1. Open the board using Win + W.
  2. Click the + (Add widgets) button at the top of the panel to select new widgets from supported apps.
  3. Click your profile icon in the upper-right corner to manage your Microsoft Start interests, taskbar widget behaviors, and feed personalization.
