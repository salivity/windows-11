How to Convert Units in Windows 11 Calculator

The built-in Windows 11 Calculator app includes powerful conversion tools that extend far beyond standard arithmetic. This guide explains how to access these built-in converters to quickly change between different units of currency, volume, and temperature directly on your PC without needing third-party software.

Accessing the Converter Modes

To use any converter in Windows 11, you must first switch the calculator mode:

  1. Open the Calculator app from the Start menu or by pressing Win + S and typing Calculator.
  2. Click the Navigation Menu (the three horizontal lines or "hamburger" icon) in the top-left corner.
  3. Scroll down to the Converter section to find Currency, Volume, Temperature, and other measurement categories.

How to Convert Currency

  1. Click the Navigation Menu and select Currency.
  2. Select your base currency using the drop-down menu on the top field.
  3. Select your target currency using the drop-down menu on the bottom field.
  4. Type the amount you want to convert in the top field.
  5. The converted value will automatically appear in the bottom field based on real-time exchange rates.

Note: An active internet connection is required to fetch the most recent exchange rates.

How to Convert Volume

  1. Click the Navigation Menu and select Volume.
  2. Click the top drop-down menu to select your source unit (such as Gallons, Liters, Cups, or Fluid Ounces).
  3. Click the bottom drop-down menu to select the target unit you want to convert into.
  4. Enter the quantity in the top field. The result will instantly display in the bottom field, along with equivalent everyday measurements (like fluid ounces or tablespoons) underneath.

How to Convert Temperature

  1. Click the Navigation Menu and select Temperature.
  2. Set the top unit to your starting scale (Fahrenheit, Celsius, or Kelvin).
  3. Set the bottom unit to your desired target scale.
  4. Input the temperature value in the top field. The calculator will immediately display the converted temperature in the bottom field.
