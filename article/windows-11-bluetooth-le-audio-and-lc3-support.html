Windows 11 Bluetooth LE Audio and LC3 Support

Windows 11 natively supports Bluetooth Low Energy (LE) Audio and the Low Complexity Communication Codec (LC3), delivering enhanced wireless sound quality, lower power consumption, and reduced latency. This article explains how Windows 11 integrates these next-generation audio standards, the practical benefits they provide, and the necessary system requirements to use them.

What is Bluetooth LE Audio and LC3 in Windows 11?

Bluetooth LE Audio is an updated architecture for wireless sound transmission built on the Bluetooth Low Energy standard. At its core is LC3 (Low Complexity Communication Codec), an audio codec designed to replace the decades-old SBC (Subband Codec).

Windows 11 integrates the LC3 codec directly into its audio processing pipeline. LC3 compresses audio more efficiently than legacy codecs, allowing the operating system to stream high-fidelity 24-bit/48kHz sound at roughly half the bitrate of standard Bluetooth classic audio.

Key Features Supported by Windows 11

1. Superior Power Efficiency

Because LC3 requires lower bitrates to achieve high audio quality, both the PC's Bluetooth radio and connected accessories consume significantly less power. This extends battery life on laptops, wireless earbuds, and compatible hearing aids.

2. Multi-Stream Audio

Legacy Bluetooth typically sends a single audio stream to one earbud, which then relays the signal to the other. Windows 11 with Bluetooth LE Audio supports true Multi-Stream Audio, allowing the PC to transmit independent, synchronized audio streams directly to each individual earbud. This eliminates relay latency and improves stereo imaging.

3. Auracast Broadcast Audio

Windows 11 includes framework support for Auracast, an LE Audio feature that allows a single PC to broadcast an audio stream to an unlimited number of nearby Bluetooth receivers simultaneously, such as multiple pairs of headphones or assistive listening devices.

4. Better Communication and Gaming Audio

Traditional Bluetooth switches to a low-fidelity mono profile (HFP/HSP) whenever a microphone is activated for voice calls. Bluetooth LE Audio allows Windows 11 to maintain high-quality stereo audio playback while simultaneously recording microphone input with minimal latency.

System Requirements for Bluetooth LE Audio on Windows 11

To take advantage of Bluetooth LE Audio and LC3 in Windows 11, both the system and the audio peripheral must meet specific hardware and driver criteria:

  • Operating System: Windows 11 version 22H2 (KB5026446) or later.
  • PC Bluetooth Hardware: A Bluetooth 5.2 or newer controller that explicitly supports LE Audio and Isochronous Channels at the hardware and firmware levels.
  • Device Drivers: Updated OEM Bluetooth radio and audio drivers supporting Windows LE Audio endpoints.
  • Audio Peripherals: Earbuds, headphones, or hearing aids certified for Bluetooth LE Audio and LC3 decoding.

How to Use Bluetooth LE Audio on Windows 11

Windows 11 handles Bluetooth LE Audio connections automatically:

  1. Open Settings on Windows 11 and select Bluetooth & devices.
  2. Ensure Bluetooth is turned on.
  3. Select Add device and place your LE Audio-compatible headphones into pairing mode.
  4. Choose your device from the list to pair.

Once connected, Windows 11 automatically detects LE Audio support and negotiates the connection using the LC3 codec without requiring manual codec selection.
