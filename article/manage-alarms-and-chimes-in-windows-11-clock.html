Manage Alarms and Chimes in Windows 11 Clock

The Windows 11 Clock app serves as a central hub for time management, allowing users to configure daily alarms, establish recurring schedules, and customize notification tones to stay on track throughout the day. This guide covers how to create and manage daily alarms, customize chime sounds, and ensure notification settings are properly configured so your alarms trigger reliably.

How to Create and Set Daily Alarms

  1. Open the Clock App: Press the Windows Key, type Clock, and press Enter.
  2. Navigate to Alarms: In the left sidebar, select the Alarm tab (clock icon).
  3. Add a New Alarm: Click the Add an alarm (+) button in the bottom-right corner of the window.
  4. Set the Time: Adjust the hour, minute, and AM/PM settings to your preferred alert time.
  5. Name the Alarm: Enter a descriptive label in the Alarm name field to easily identify its purpose.
  6. Set Daily Recurrence: Check the box next to Repeat alarm, then click on every day of the week (Sunday through Saturday) to make it a daily alarm. Selected days will be highlighted.

How to Customize Alarm Chime Tones

  1. Select a Sound: In the alarm creation or editing window, locate the Chime dropdown menu.
  2. Preview Tones: Choose from the list of built-in Windows tones (such as Chimes, Xylophone, Jingle, Ascending, or Breeze). Click the play icon next to any tone to preview how it sounds.
  3. Set Snooze Duration: Click the Snooze time dropdown to define how long the alarm pauses when snoozed (options range from 5 minutes to 1 hour).
  4. Save Changes: Click the Save (disk) icon at the bottom of the window to apply your settings.

How to Edit, Toggle, and Delete Alarms

  • Toggle On/Off: Use the toggle switch located in the top-right corner of any alarm card to quickly activate or deactivate it without deleting your configuration.
  • Edit an Existing Alarm: Click directly anywhere on an alarm card to reopen the configuration panel and change the time, chime sound, or recurring days.
  • Delete an Alarm: Hover over or select the alarm card and click the Delete (trash can) icon in the bottom-right corner.

Essential Tips for Reliable Alarms

  • Keep the PC Awake: Standard Windows alarms will only sound if your computer is awake or supports Modern Standby. To prevent missed alarms, check that your power settings do not put the device into deep sleep or hibernation during scheduled alarm times.
  • Enable Notification Permissions: Ensure the Clock app has permission to send alerts. Go to Settings > System > Notifications, make sure notifications are turned on, and verify that the Clock app is allowed to deliver banners and sounds.
