How to Hide or Show Copilot on Windows 11 Taskbar

Windows 11 includes an integrated Microsoft Copilot button directly on the taskbar, offering quick access to AI-powered assistance. However, if you prefer a cleaner workspace or need to restore an icon that has disappeared, you can easily toggle this feature on or off. This guide provides a clear, step-by-step walkthrough to help you show or hide the Copilot button using Windows Settings.

How to Toggle the Copilot Icon via Taskbar Settings

The fastest way to manage the Copilot button is directly through the Windows 11 Taskbar settings menu.

  1. Open Taskbar Settings: Right-click an empty area on your taskbar and select Taskbar settings. Alternatively, press Windows Key + I to open Settings, click on Personalization in the left sidebar, and then select Taskbar.
  2. Locate the Copilot Setting: In the Taskbar menu, look under the Taskbar items section at the top. You will see a list of default Windows features, including Search, Task view, Widgets, and Copilot.
  3. Toggle the Button:
    • To hide the Copilot icon, turn the toggle switch next to Copilot to Off.
    • To show the Copilot icon, turn the toggle switch next to Copilot to On.

The change will take effect immediately, and the Copilot icon will disappear from or appear on your taskbar without requiring a system restart.

What to Do If the Copilot Toggle Is Missing

If the Copilot option is not visible in your Taskbar settings:

  • Update Windows 11: Go to Settings > Windows Update and click Check for updates to ensure you have the latest build.
  • Regional Availability: Microsoft Copilot features vary depending on regional regulations and licensing.
  • Keyboard Shortcut: Even with the taskbar icon hidden, you can still open Copilot at any time by pressing the Windows Key + C keyboard shortcut.
