Restore Windows 11 Cloud Backup During Initial Setup

Restoring your cloud backup during the initial setup of a new Windows 11 computer allows you to seamlessly transfer your desktop preferences, settings, credentials, and pinned applications from an old device. By signing in with your Microsoft account during the Out-of-Box Experience (OOBE), Windows Backup automatically detects your saved profiles, letting you pick up right where you left off with minimal manual configuration.

Prerequisites Before You Begin

  • Ensure your previous PC was backed up using the Windows Backup app to your OneDrive/Microsoft account.
  • Have your Microsoft account email and password ready.
  • Ensure your new device is connected to a reliable internet connection (Wi-Fi or Ethernet).

Step-by-Step Guide to Restore Your Profile

  1. Power on the Device Turn on your new Windows 11 PC to launch the initial setup wizard (Out-of-Box Experience).

  2. Select Region and Keyboard Choose your correct country/region, keyboard layout, and secondary keyboard preferences when prompted.

  3. Connect to the Internet Select your Wi-Fi network and enter the password, or plug in an Ethernet cable. An internet connection is mandatory to retrieve cloud backups.

  4. Sign In to Your Microsoft Account When prompted, sign in using the exact same Microsoft account (MSA) credentials associated with the backup from your previous PC.

  5. Choose Your Backup Profile After authentication, the setup wizard will display a screen titled "Restore from your PC" or "Welcome back!" listing available cloud backups associated with your account.

    • If you have multiple backups, click "Restore from another PC" to select the specific machine profile you wish to restore.
    • Verify the name of the PC and the date of the backup.

  6. Confirm the Restore Process Click "Restore from this PC" to apply the configuration. If you prefer a completely clean slate instead, you would select "Set up as a new device."

  7. Configure Security and Privacy

    • Set up Windows Hello (Face, Fingerprint, or PIN) as prompted.
    • Review and accept your preferred privacy settings.

  8. Finalize Setup Windows will finalize the installation. Once the desktop loads, your personalized theme, system settings, desktop icons, and supported Microsoft Store applications will automatically sync and download in the background.
