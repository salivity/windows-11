Voice Recorder vs Sound Recorder in Windows 11

In Windows 11, Microsoft replaced the legacy Voice Recorder app with a redesigned and modernized Sound Recorder. While both applications serve the primary function of capturing audio from your microphone, Sound Recorder introduces a substantial overhaul in visual design, audio format support, quality settings, and playback controls compared to its predecessor.

User Interface and Visual Design

Voice Recorder featured the older Windows 10 visual language, characterized by flat menus, a minimalist layout, and a static interface.

Sound Recorder adopts the modern Windows 11 Fluent Design system. It includes support for system dark and light modes, rounded corners, and a dynamic waveform visualization that animates in real time while recording and during playback.

Audio Format and Export Options

One of the largest functional differences lies in supported file formats:

  • Voice Recorder: Exclusively saved recordings in the .m4a (AAC) format, requiring third-party tools if you needed other file types.
  • Sound Recorder: Allows you to choose your preferred output format from within the app settings. Supported formats include M4A, MP3, WMA, FLAC, and WAV.

Audio Quality Configuration

Voice Recorder offered no built-in settings to adjust audio bitrate or fidelity.

Sound Recorder includes a dedicated settings menu where users can customize the recording quality. You can select between:

  • Auto
  • Best
  • High
  • Medium
  • Low

Input Device Selection

In Voice Recorder, changing your input microphone required navigating through the main Windows Settings app or Control Panel to alter the default system recording device.

Sound Recorder includes an in-app device switcher. You can view, select, and switch between multiple connected microphones or input sources directly from the recording screen without leaving the application.

Playback and Editing Capabilities

Both applications allow users to set markers within recordings to highlight important moments, as well as perform basic audio trimming. However, Sound Recorder improves playback functionality by adding variable playback speeds, allowing users to listen to recordings at speeds ranging from 0.25x to 2x.

Summary

Sound Recorder is not a separate side-by-side utility, but rather the official successor to Voice Recorder delivered via Microsoft Store updates. It retains the straightforward nature of Voice Recorder while eliminating its limitations regarding audio formats, quality controls, and hardware selection.
