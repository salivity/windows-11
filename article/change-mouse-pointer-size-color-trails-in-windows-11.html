Change Mouse Pointer Size, Color & Trails in Windows 11

Customizing your mouse cursor in Windows 11 can significantly improve visibility, reduce eye strain, and add personal flair to your workspace. This guide explains how to adjust your mouse pointer's size and color using the Windows 11 Accessibility settings, as well as how to enable visual indicator trails and pointer-locating features through the classic mouse properties menu.

How to Change Mouse Pointer Size and Color

Windows 11 includes built-in accessibility options that make resizing and recoloring your pointer simple.

  1. Press Windows Key + I to open the Settings app.
  2. Select Accessibility from the left-hand navigation pane.
  3. In the Vision section, click on Mouse pointer and touch.
  4. Use the Size slider to increase or decrease the size of your cursor in real time (from 1 to 15).
  5. Choose a style under the Mouse pointer style section:
    • White: The default solid white pointer with a black border.
    • Black: A solid black pointer with a white border.
    • Inverted: An inverted pointer that dynamically flips colors based on the background.
    • Custom: Allows you to select from recommended bright colors or choose your own custom color via a color picker.

How to Enable Mouse Pointer Trails

Mouse trails create a visual stream of ghost pointers behind your cursor, making it easier to track motion across high-resolution or multiple displays.

  1. Open Settings (Windows Key + I).
  2. Go to Bluetooth & devices and select Mouse.
  3. Scroll down and click on Additional mouse settings under the Related settings section. This opens the Mouse Properties dialog box.
  4. Click on the Pointer Options tab.
  5. Under the Visibility section, check the box for Display pointer trails.
  6. Adjust the slider between Short and Long to set the desired trail length.
  7. Click Apply, then OK to save your changes.

How to Enable the Pointer Location Indicator

If you frequently lose track of your cursor on the screen, you can enable a circular visual indicator that appears whenever you tap the Ctrl key.

  1. In the same Mouse Properties window (accessed via Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Mouse > Additional mouse settings), navigate to the Pointer Options tab.
  2. Under the Visibility section, check the box labeled Show location of pointer when I press the CTRL key.
  3. Click Apply and OK.

Now, pressing the Ctrl key on your keyboard will instantly animate a shrinking ring around the cursor to reveal its position.
