Set Separate Screen Timeouts in Windows 11

Managing separate screen timeout rules for battery and plugged-in modes in Windows 11 allows you to conserve battery life while on the move without compromising productivity when connected to power. This guide provides clear, step-by-step instructions to configure independent display turn-off timers through the Windows 11 Settings app.

How to Adjust Screen Timeout Settings

Follow these steps to customize when your screen turns off based on your device's power source:

  1. Open Settings: Press the shortcut Windows Key + I on your keyboard, or right-click the Start button and select Settings.
  2. Navigate to Power Settings: In the left sidebar, click on System, then select Power & battery (labeled simply as Power on desktop computers) from the right pane.
  3. Expand Screen & Sleep Options: Click on the Screen and sleep section to expand the dropdown menus.
  4. Set Battery Timeout: Locate the setting labeled On battery power, turn off my screen after and click the dropdown menu to select your desired duration (e.g., 3 minutes, 5 minutes, or 10 minutes).
  5. Set Plugged-In Timeout: Locate the setting labeled When plugged in, turn off my screen after and select your preferred duration (e.g., 15 minutes, 30 minutes, or Never).

Additional Tips for Power Management

  • Sleep Timers: Directly beneath the screen timeout settings, you can also configure independent sleep timers for On battery power and When plugged in to determine when the entire PC goes into a low-power state.
  • Instant Application: Changes take effect immediately; there is no need to click a save button or restart your computer.
  • Power Modes: You can further optimize performance and battery consumption by adjusting the Power mode setting located on the same Power & battery page.
