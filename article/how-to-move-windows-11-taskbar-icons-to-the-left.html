How to Move Windows 11 Taskbar Icons to the Left

Windows 11 features a centered Taskbar by default, which is a major visual departure from previous versions of the operating system. If you prefer the classic layout found in Windows 10 and earlier versions, you can easily change the icon alignment back to the left side through the native personalization settings. This guide provides a straightforward, step-by-step walkthrough to help you reposition your Taskbar icons.

Steps to Move Taskbar Icons to the Left

  1. Open Taskbar Settings: Right-click on any empty space on your Taskbar and select Taskbar settings. Alternatively, press Windows Key + I to open Settings, select Personalization from the left sidebar, and click on Taskbar.
  2. Expand Taskbar Behaviors: Scroll down to the bottom of the Taskbar settings page and click on the Taskbar behaviors section to expand its options.
  3. Change Alignment: Locate the Taskbar alignment setting at the top of the expanded list. Click the dropdown menu next to it (which is set to Center by default) and select Left.

Once selected, your Start button and pinned application icons will immediately shift to the bottom-left corner of your screen without requiring a system restart. If you ever want to revert to the default centered design, follow the same steps and select Center from the dropdown menu.
