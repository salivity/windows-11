How to Set Up a VPN in Windows 11

Setting up a Virtual Private Network (VPN) in Windows 11 allows you to secure your internet connection, protect sensitive data, and access private network resources remotely. This guide provides a straightforward, step-by-step walkthrough for configuring the built-in Windows 11 VPN client, entering your connection details, and managing your VPN settings.

Prerequisites

Before starting, ensure you have the following information from your network administrator or VPN service provider:

  • VPN Provider: Typically "Windows (built-in)".
  • Connection Name: A friendly name of your choice (e.g., "Work VPN").
  • Server Name or Address: The domain name (e.g., vpn.example.com) or IP address of the VPN server.
  • VPN Type: The protocol used (e.g., IKEv2, SSTP, L2TP/IPsec with pre-shared key, or Automatic).
  • Sign-in Info: The method used to authenticate (Username and password, Smart card, Certificate, or One-time password) along with your credentials.

Step 1: Open VPN Settings

  1. Press Windows Key + I to open the Settings app.
  2. In the left sidebar, click Network & internet.
  3. Select VPN from the menu on the right.

Step 2: Add a VPN Connection

  1. Click the Add VPN button located at the top of the window.
  2. In the configuration window that appears, fill in the required fields:
    • VPN provider: Select Windows (built-in) from the dropdown.
    • Connection name: Enter a name to identify this network.
    • Server name or address: Enter the hostname or IP address provided by your VPN host.
    • VPN type: Select your specific protocol (e.g., IKEv2 or L2TP/IPsec) or leave it on Automatic.
    • Type of sign-in info: Select your authentication method (most commonly User name and password).
    • User name (optional): Enter your username.
    • Password (optional): Enter your password.
  3. Check Remember my sign-in info if you do not want to enter your credentials every time you connect.
  4. Click Save.

Step 3: Connect to Your VPN

Once the profile is created, you can connect using either of the following methods:

  • From Settings: Go to Settings > Network & internet > VPN, locate your profile, and click Connect.
  • From the Taskbar: Click the Network, Volume, and Battery quick settings cluster on your taskbar, click the VPN icon, choose your connection, and select Connect.

Step 4: Configure Advanced Settings

To modify advanced options such as proxy settings or metered connection rules:

  1. Go to Settings > Network & internet > VPN.
  2. Click the arrow next to your VPN connection name.
  3. Under More connection options, click Edit to update your server address or sign-in credentials, or adjust the Proxy settings and Metered connection toggles as required by your network environment.

Step 5: Disconnect or Remove a VPN Profile

  • To Disconnect: Open the VPN menu from the taskbar or Settings and click Disconnect.
  • To Remove: Navigate to Settings > Network & internet > VPN, click the arrow next to the VPN profile, and click Remove. Confirm the deletion when prompted.
