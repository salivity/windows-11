How to Enable Windows Sonic Spatial Audio in Windows 11

Windows Sonic for Headphones is a built-in spatial sound solution in Windows 11 that creates a virtual 3D surround sound environment, significantly improving your gaming, movie, and music experiences. This guide provides direct, step-by-step instructions to enable Windows Sonic using both the Windows 11 Settings app and the classic Sound Control Panel.

Method 1: Enable Windows Sonic via Windows 11 Settings

  1. Connect your headphones to your Windows 11 PC.
  2. Open the Settings app by pressing Windows Key + I.
  3. Select System from the left-hand menu, then click on Sound on the right.
  4. Under the Output section, click on your connected headphones to open the Properties page.
  5. Scroll down to the Spatial audio section.
  6. Click the drop-down menu next to Type and select Windows Sonic for Headphones.

Spatial audio will activate immediately for your selected output device.

Method 2: Enable Windows Sonic via Sound Control Panel

  1. Press Windows Key + R to open the Run dialog box.
  2. Type mmsys.cpl and press Enter to open the classic Sound settings.
  3. Under the Playback tab, locate your headphones, right-click them, and select Properties.
  4. Navigate to the Spatial sound tab at the top of the properties window.
  5. Under the Spatial sound format section, select Windows Sonic for Headphones from the drop-down menu.
  6. Click Apply, then click OK to save the changes.

Important Considerations for Windows Sonic

  • Mono Audio: Ensure that Mono Audio is turned off. If Mono Audio is enabled in Windows 11 Settings (under Accessibility > Audio), Spatial Audio cannot function and the option will be grayed out.
  • Exclusive Mode: For standard applications, leave default sample rates intact. Most games and modern media players will automatically leverage the 3D audio format once enabled.
