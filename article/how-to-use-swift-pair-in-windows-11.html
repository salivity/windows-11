How to Use Swift Pair in Windows 11

Swift Pair is a built-in Windows 11 feature designed to streamline the Bluetooth pairing process by reducing the number of steps required to connect compatible peripherals. Instead of navigating deeply into the Windows settings menu, Swift Pair generates a prompt on your screen as soon as a supported device enters pairing mode nearby. This guide explains how to enable Swift Pair on your Windows 11 PC, prepare your peripheral, and complete the connection instantly.

Step 1: Enable Swift Pair in Windows 11

Before you can use Swift Pair, you must ensure the feature and Bluetooth are active on your PC.

  1. Open Settings by pressing Windows Key + I.
  2. Click on Bluetooth & devices in the left sidebar.
  3. Ensure the main Bluetooth toggle is switched to On.
  4. Click on Devices.
  5. Scroll down to the Device settings section.
  6. Check the box or toggle the switch next to Show notifications to connect using Swift Pair to turn it On.

Step 2: Put Your Bluetooth Peripheral in Pairing Mode

For Windows to detect the accessory via Swift Pair, the peripheral must be actively broadcasting its pairing signal.

  1. Turn on your Bluetooth accessory (e.g., mouse, keyboard, headphones).
  2. Press and hold the Pairing button on the device until the LED indicator begins flashing rapidly. Refer to your device’s manual if you are unsure how to trigger pairing mode.

Step 3: Connect via the Pop-Up Notification

Once the accessory is in pairing mode and placed close to your computer:

  1. Bring the peripheral near your Windows 11 PC.
  2. A toast notification will appear in the lower-right corner of your screen showing the device name and an image.
  3. Click the Connect button on the notification.
  4. Wait a few seconds for Windows to complete the setup. A follow-up notification will confirm that the device is ready to use.

Troubleshooting Swift Pair

  • No Notification Appears: Ensure the peripheral supports Microsoft Swift Pair (not all Bluetooth devices do). If unsupported, connect manually via Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Add device.
  • Distance: Move the peripheral closer to the PC antenna (typically within 1 to 3 feet during initial pairing).
  • Focus Assist / Do Not Disturb: Ensure Do Not Disturb mode is turned off in Windows 11, as it can suppress the pairing pop-up notification.
