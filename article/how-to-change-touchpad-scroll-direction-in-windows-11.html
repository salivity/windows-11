How to Change Touchpad Scroll Direction in Windows 11

Windows 11 allows users to easily customize their precision touchpad behavior, including changing the scrolling direction to match personal preferences. Whether you prefer the traditional Windows scrolling method or the "natural" smartphone-style reverse scrolling, you can switch between these modes directly from the Windows 11 Settings app without installing third-party software.

Steps to Change Touchpad Scrolling Direction

  1. Open Settings: Press Windows Key + I on your keyboard, or right-click the Start button and select Settings.
  2. Navigate to Touchpad Settings: In the left sidebar, click on Bluetooth & devices, then scroll down and click Touchpad on the right pane.
  3. Open Scroll & Zoom Options: Click on the Scroll & zoom section to expand its settings.
  4. Change Scrolling Direction: Locate the Scrolling direction drop-down menu and select your preferred behavior:
    • Down motion scrolls down: This is the traditional Windows behavior. Swiping two fingers downward moves the scrollbar down, shifting the page view downward.
    • Down motion scrolls up: This enables "natural" or reverse scrolling (similar to macOS, iOS, and Android). Swiping two fingers downward moves the content itself downward, shifting the page view upward.

The setting applies immediately without requiring a system restart.

Troubleshooting Missing Options

If the Scroll & zoom or Scrolling direction options do not appear in the Settings app, your laptop may not be using a Windows Precision Touchpad. In this case:

  • Check the top of the Touchpad settings page for the text: "Your PC has a precision touchpad."
  • If this text is missing, open your device manufacturer's dedicated control panel (such as Synaptics, ELAN, or Dell Touchpad settings) in the Windows Control Panel or System Tray to adjust gesture settings.
