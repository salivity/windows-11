How to Wipe Windows 11 for Resale or Recycling

Preparing a Windows 11 computer for resale or recycling requires completely removing your personal files, de-authorizing licenses, and securely wiping the internal storage drive so your data cannot be recovered by the next user. This guide outlines the essential steps to safely transition your PC, from backing up your files to performing a secure factory reset that leaves the system ready for its new owner.

Step 1: Back Up Your Files

Before erasing anything, save any files, browser bookmarks, and game saves you wish to keep:

  • Copy important data to an external hard drive or USB flash drive.
  • Alternatively, sync your folders using cloud storage platforms like OneDrive, Google Drive, or Dropbox.
  • Record any application license keys or software credentials you will need on a new device.

Step 2: Sign Out of Accounts and De-authorize Software

To prevent activation issues and protect your personal subscriptions:

  • Open installed paid software (such as Microsoft Office, Adobe Creative Cloud, or specialized software) and deactivate or sign out of the licenses.
  • Remove your computer from your Microsoft account online by logging into account.microsoft.com/devices and selecting Remove device.

Step 3: Perform a Secure Factory Reset

Windows 11 includes a built-in reset feature capable of securely wiping the storage drive:

  1. Open Settings by pressing Windows Key + I.
  2. Navigate to System and select Recovery.
  3. Next to Reset this PC, click the Reset PC button.
  4. Choose Remove everything in the popup window.
  5. Select Local reinstall (faster) or Cloud download (downloads a fresh copy of Windows).
  6. Under Additional settings, click Change settings.
  7. Toggle Clean data? to Yes. This writes over the storage drive to prevent unauthorized file recovery.
  8. Click Confirm, then select Next and click Reset.

Step 4: Finalize for the New User

The reset process may take from 30 minutes to several hours depending on the size and speed of your drive. Once the reset finishes, the computer will reboot to the initial Windows setup screen (the Out-of-Box Experience).

Do not proceed with the setup. Instead, press and hold the power button to shut down the machine. The next person who turns on the PC will be greeted with the initial configuration screen, allowing them to set up their own user profile securely.
