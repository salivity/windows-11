Enable Dolby Atmos and DTS Sound in Windows 11

Spatial audio enhances your gaming, movie, and music experience on Windows 11 by creating a virtual three-dimensional sound environment using standard headphones or home theater setups. This guide provides a straightforward walkthrough on how to install, configure, and manage premium spatial sound formats like Dolby Atmos for Headphones and DTS Headphone:X, alongside the built-in Windows Sonic platform.

Step 1: Install the Necessary Spatial Sound Apps

Windows 11 includes "Windows Sonic for Headphones" by default, but third-party formats like Dolby Atmos and DTS Headphone:X require companion applications from the Microsoft Store.

  1. Open the Microsoft Store app from your Start Menu.
  2. Search for:
    • Dolby Access (for Dolby Atmos)
    • DTS Sound Unbound (for DTS Headphone:X)
  3. Click Install or Get to download the desired app.
  4. Launch the installed app and follow the on-screen prompts to activate a free trial or purchase a license.

Step 2: Enable Spatial Sound in Windows 11 Settings

Once your preferred app is installed and licensed, you can activate the format through the main Windows Settings.

  1. Press Windows Key + I to open Settings.
  2. Navigate to System > Sound.
  3. Under the Output section, click on your active playback device (headphones or speakers).
  4. Scroll down to the Spatial sound section.
  5. Click the dropdown menu next to Type and select your preferred format:
    • Windows Sonic for Headphones
    • Dolby Atmos for Headphones (or Dolby Atmos for Home Theater)
    • DTS Headphone:X
  6. Windows will immediately apply the spatial sound processing to your selected output.

Step 3: Quick Switching via the Taskbar

You can switch spatial audio profiles or turn them off directly from the Windows 11 taskbar without opening the full Settings menu.

  1. Right-click the Speaker icon in the system tray (bottom-right corner).
  2. Select Sound settings.
  3. Alternatively, click the Quick Settings panel (Volume/Network cluster), click the Select a sound output icon next to the volume slider, and configure your output properties directly.

Step 4: Fine-Tuning Audio Profiles

Both Dolby Access and DTS Sound Unbound include built-in equalizers and profile presets (such as Game, Movie, Music, and Voice).

  • For Dolby Atmos: Open the Dolby Access app, go to the Settings tab, and choose a preset tailored to your current media type, or enable "Performance Mode" to prioritize directional cues in competitive games.
  • For DTS Headphone:X: Open DTS Sound Unbound to select specific headphone models for calibrated frequency responses, or toggle between "Spacious" and "Balanced" modes.
