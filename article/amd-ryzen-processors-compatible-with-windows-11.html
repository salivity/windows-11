AMD Ryzen Processors Compatible with Windows 11

Microsoft established strict hardware requirements for Windows 11 to enhance platform security, stability, and performance. For AMD systems, official support generally begins with the Zen+ and Zen 2 microarchitectures, excluding most first-generation Zen chips. This guide breaks down every AMD Ryzen processor family officially certified by Microsoft to run Windows 11 without requiring bypass methods or manual configuration workarounds.

Overview of Windows 11 AMD Hardware Requirements

To officially support Windows 11, AMD processors must meet specific architectural standards:

  • Architecture: 64-bit with support for SSE4.2 and modern instruction sets.
  • Security: Support for Trusted Platform Module (TPM) version 2.0 (via AMD fTPM or a dedicated chip) and UEFI Secure Boot.
  • Generation Baseline: Most Ryzen 2000-series (Zen+) desktop chips and virtually all Ryzen 3000-series and newer processors.

Supported Desktop Ryzen Families

AMD Ryzen 2000 Series (Zen+)

Most second-generation Ryzen desktop processors built on the 12nm Zen+ architecture are supported:

  • Ryzen 3: 2300X
  • Ryzen 5: 2600, 2600X, 2600E
  • Ryzen 7: 2700, 2700X, 2700E (Note: Ryzen 3 2200G and Ryzen 5 2400G are based on first-gen Zen architecture and are not officially supported).

AMD Ryzen 3000 Series (Zen 2)

All standard 3000-series desktop processors and APUs are supported:

  • Ryzen 3: 3100, 3300X, 3200G, 3200GE
  • Ryzen 5: 3500, 3500X, 3600, 3600X, 3600XT, 3400G, 3400GE
  • Ryzen 7: 3700X, 3800X, 3800XT
  • Ryzen 9: 3900, 3900X, 3900XT, 3950X

AMD Ryzen 4000 Series (Zen 2 Desktop APUs)

  • Ryzen 3: 4100, 4300G, 4300GE
  • Ryzen 5: 4500, 4600G, 4600GE
  • Ryzen 7: 4700G, 4700GE

AMD Ryzen 5000 Series (Zen 3)

  • Ryzen 3: 5100, 5300G
  • Ryzen 5: 5500, 5600, 5600X, 5600X3D, 5600G, 5600GT
  • Ryzen 7: 5700, 5700X, 5700X3D, 5800X, 5800X3D, 5700G
  • Ryzen 9: 5900, 5900X, 5900XT, 5950X

AMD Ryzen 7000 Series (Zen 4)

  • Ryzen 5: 7500F, 7600, 7600X
  • Ryzen 7: 7700, 7700X, 7800X3D
  • Ryzen 9: 7900, 7900X, 7900X3D, 7950X, 7950X3D

AMD Ryzen 8000 Series (Zen 4 Desktop APUs)

  • Ryzen 3: 8300G
  • Ryzen 5: 8500G, 8600G
  • Ryzen 7: 8700G, 8700F

AMD Ryzen 9000 Series (Zen 5)

  • Ryzen 5: 9600X
  • Ryzen 7: 9700X, 9800X3D
  • Ryzen 9: 9900X, 9950X

Supported Mobile and Laptop Ryzen Families

  • Ryzen 3000 Mobile Series: Zen+ and Zen 2 laptop chips (e.g., Ryzen 3 3300U, Ryzen 5 3500U, Ryzen 7 3700U).
  • Ryzen 4000 Mobile Series: Zen 2 laptop chips (e.g., Ryzen 5 4600H/U, Ryzen 7 4800H/U).
  • Ryzen 5000 Mobile Series: Zen 2 and Zen 3 laptop chips (e.g., Ryzen 5 5600H/U, Ryzen 7 5800H/U).
  • Ryzen 6000 Mobile Series: Zen 3+ laptop chips (e.g., Ryzen 5 6600H/U, Ryzen 7 6800H/U, Ryzen 9 6900HX).
  • Ryzen 7000 Mobile Series: Mendocino, Barcelo-R, Rembrandt-R, Phoenix, and Dragon Range series.
  • Ryzen 8040 Mobile Series: Hawk Point processors with integrated NPU acceleration.
  • Ryzen AI 300 Series: Zen 5 mobile processors featuring XDNA 2 AI hardware (e.g., Ryzen AI 9 HX 370).

Supported Workstation and HEDT Processors

  • Ryzen Threadripper 2000 Series: 2920X, 2950X, 2970WX, 2990WX.
  • Ryzen Threadripper 3000 Series: 3960X, 3970X, 3990X, along with the Threadripper PRO 3000WX series.
  • Ryzen Threadripper 5000 Series: Threadripper PRO 5945WX through 5995WX.
  • Ryzen Threadripper 7000 Series: Threadripper 7000X series and PRO 7000WX series.

Notable Excluded Processors

The following AMD processor families do not meet Microsoft's official compatibility list:

  • Ryzen 1000 Series: Entire original Zen lineup (e.g., Ryzen 5 1600, Ryzen 7 1800X).
  • Ryzen 2000 APUs: Ryzen 3 2200G and Ryzen 5 2400G (based on original 14nm Zen architecture).
  • Ryzen Threadripper 1000 Series: First-generation HEDT chips (e.g., 1950X).
